4. Limits you know

  • POWER is a regional grid value, not a measurement of one plot. Confirm the exact grid size in POWER's own metadata before quoting it.
  • The coordinates are a planning point and the pilot plot boundary is a placeholder until the team confirms the real plot.
  • The 10-year monthly averages are a short record and include a few extreme storms.
  • Rain from one month of comparison (Sept 2024) differed between POWER and IMERG by up to about 145 mm on a single day. Neither is ground truth.
  • August 2026 shows about 1,362 mm in POWER. That is unverified and needs rain-gauge data.
  • null means a missing value, not zero.
